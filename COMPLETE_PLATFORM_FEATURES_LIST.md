# EventEase Platform: Complete Features Inventory

This document provides a unified list of all features across the Admin, Vendor, and Customer sides of the EventEase platform.

---

## 🛡️ 1. Administrative Features (Control Center)
*The administrative suite is designed for platform oversight, financial management, and system security.*

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
*   **Marketplace Metrics**: Real-time GMV, Revenue, and Booking trends.
*   **Product Usage**: Active user tracking (MAU/DAU) and retention analytics.
*   **Vendor Performance**: Ranking vendors by rating, response time, and sales.
*   **Category Analysis**: Identifying high-growth service categories.

### 👥 User & Vendor Management
*   **Unified Management**: List, search, and manage statuses for all Users and Vendors.
*   **Document Verification**: Automated/Manual review of vendor licenses and insurance.
*   **Vendor Onboarding**: Administrative flow for manual vendor creation and approval.
*   **Security Controls**: Password resets and profile editing for all users.

### 💰 Financials & Monetization
*   **Transaction Audit**: Full history of every payment made on the platform.
*   **Commission Engine**: Global and vendor-specific commission rate overrides.
*   **Installment Oversight**: Monitoring multi-vendor payment plans and schedules.
*   **Subscription Tiers**: Managing membership levels for both vendors and customers.
*   **Ad Management**: Full control over platform-wide banner and native ads.

### 🛠️ Operations & Support
*   **Service Approval**: Detailed review workflow for new vendor service listings.
*   **Helpdesk Dashboard**: Centralized support ticket management for platform issues.
*   **Dispute Moderation**: Handling review appeals and transaction disputes.
*   **Region Management**: Configuring active cities and operational areas.

## 🏪 2. Vendor Features (Business Suite)
*Tools for vendors to manage their digital storefront, services, and growth.*

### 📈 Storefront & Sales
*   **Business Dashboard**: Daily overview of earnings, bookings, and customer inquiries.
*   **Service Wizard**: Multi-step builder for products, services, and complex packages.
*   **Availability Engine**: Calendar-based slot management and blackout dates.
*   **Inventory Tracking**: Real-time stock management for physical goods.
*   **Shop Customization**: Branding, theme colors, and portfolio showcase.

### 📅 Booking & Operations
*   **Booking Management**: Real-time request handling and status transitions.
*   **Quotation Builder**: Professional digital quotes for custom customer requests.
*   **Amendment Tracking**: Managing changes to existing bookings with audit trails.
*   **QR Marketing**: Unique shop QR codes for offline customer engagement.

### 💰 Finance & Growth
*   **Earnings Dashboard**: Payout tracking and revenue forecasting.
*   **Installment Config**: Setting custom deposit and payment plan rules.
*   **Promotion Center**: Creating discount codes, coupons, and flash sales.
*   **Ad Manager**: Buying and tracking ad placements on the customer app.
*   **CRM & Segmentation**: Categorizing customers for targeted marketing campaigns.

### 🤝 Networking & Support
*   **Vendor Groups**: Join regional or service-based communities for coordination.
*   **B2B Networking**: Finding and collaborating with complementary vendors.
*   **Partnership Requests**: Sending and receiving B2B collaboration invites.
*   **Appointment Management**: Handling 1-on-1 consultations and site visits.
*   **Review Responses**: Monitoring and replying to customer feedback.
*   **Staff Management**: Managing multi-user access to the vendor dashboard.

## 👰 3. Customer Features (Planning & Shopping)
*Features designed for event hosts to discover, book, and plan their perfect event.*

### 🔍 Discovery & Marketplace
*   **Smart Search**: AI-powered search with advanced filters for price, rating, and location.
*   **Service Explorer**: Browsing 20+ categories of event services and products.
*   **Comparison Engine**: Side-by-side comparison of multiple vendors.
*   **Favorites**: Personal wishlists for vendors and specific service packages.

### 🛒 Booking & Payments
*   **Multi-Vendor Cart**: Single checkout flow for items from multiple different vendors.
*   **Payment Plans**: Integrated installment and deposit options at checkout.
*   **Universal Booking**: Real-time slot booking for venues, photographers, and more.
*   **Order Tracking**: Visual status updates for every service booked.

### 📅 Event Planning Tools
*   **Event Planner**: Interactive checklist and timeline for event coordination.
*   **Budget Tracker**: Managing estimated vs. actual costs with automatic updates.
*   **Guest List Pro**: RSVP tracking, invitations, and dietary requirement management.
*   **Group Collaboration**: Inviting co-hosts to help plan and manage events.
*   **Calendar Sync**: Integrating event dates with Google and Outlook calendars.

### 💬 Support & Reviews
*   **Real-time Chat**: Direct messaging with vendors for fast coordination.
*   **Review System**: Community-driven ratings and feedback for all services.
*   **Support Center**: Searchable FAQs and direct access to helpdesk agents.
